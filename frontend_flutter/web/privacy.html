Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Educational Use Only

MedicoHub Connect supports education, communication, questions, and follow-up. It is not an emergency medical app and does not replace direct medical consultation.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email, role, language, and optional mobile number.
  • Authentication data handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Questions, comments, replies, education content interactions, and moderation records.
  • Optional uploads you choose to attach.
  • Diagnostics needed for security, reliability, and support.

How We Use Data

We use data to operate accounts, route questions, show education material, support moderation, improve reliability, and provide user-requested features.

Advertising

Ads or sponsored content, where enabled, must be clearly labeled and must not use private medical details for targeting.

Account Deletion

Use the in-app account deletion option or contact doctornerves@gmail.com from your account email.
