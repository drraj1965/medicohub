# Microsoft Partner Center Steps

1. Open Partner Center: https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard
2. Confirm developer account type.
3. Reserve app name: `MedicoHub Connect`.
4. Create a new app submission.
5. Choose PWA/package upload once PWABuilder package is ready.
6. Add Store listing metadata.
7. Add privacy/support URLs:
   - Privacy: `https://mediconverse.web.app/privacy.html`
   - Support: `https://mediconverse.web.app/support.html`
   - Website: `https://mediconverse.web.app/`
8. Add screenshots captured from the signed-in PWA/app window, not splash-screen-only or login-screen-only images.
9. Complete age rating/content declarations.
10. Add reviewer notes:
    - Educational app, not emergency medical service.
    - Demo account credentials.
    - Any special login instructions.
11. Submit for certification.
