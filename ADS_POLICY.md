# MedicoHub Advertising Policy

## Core Rule

Ads must support the app, not become the app. They must be minimal, labeled, medically safe, and privacy-preserving.

## Allowed Placement

- Bottom of article pages.
- Between article cards after every 5 to 8 cards.
- Desktop sidebar only.
- Education or broad awareness pages.

## Forbidden Placement

- Private patient questions.
- Doctor replies.
- Sensitive medical reports.
- Login, account deletion, or emergency warning flows.
- During video playback.
- Popups, blockers, or autoplay video ads.

## Forbidden Ad Content

- Miracle cures.
- Unverified treatments.
- Gambling.
- Alcohol.
- Tobacco.
- Adult content.
- Political content.
- Misleading health products.
- Anything that looks like medical advice from MedicoHub.

## Targeting Rule

Use broad non-sensitive page context only. Examples:

- neurology education
- migraine article
- stroke awareness
- vestibular exercise page

Do not use private medical details, question content, reports, diagnoses, or patient identities.

## Disclosure

Every ad/sponsor item must say `Sponsored` or `Advertisement`.

## User Control

Users should have a setting called:

`Personalized content suggestions / ads`

Default: OFF unless legal review says otherwise.

## Admin Control

Admins must be able to disable all ads globally.
