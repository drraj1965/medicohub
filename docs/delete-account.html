Account controls

User Data Deletion Instructions

Inside the app

Open Settings or Profile & Account, then use the account deletion option where available.

Email request

If you cannot access the app, email doctornerves@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and write “Delete my MedicoHub account” in the subject.

What may remain

Some records may be retained or de-identified when needed for safety, legal, moderation, abuse-prevention, or audit reasons.
