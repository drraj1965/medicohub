MedicoHub Connect is an educational doctor-patient communication app for non-emergency questions, thread follow-up, and practical health learning.

Designed for quick, calm use

The app keeps the important paths close at hand: sign in, ask, review, and continue.

Educational use only

MedicoHub Connect is for education and communication support. It does not provide diagnosis, prescriptions, or emergency care. For urgent symptoms, contact local emergency services or your treating clinician directly.
