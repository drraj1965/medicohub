Terms of Use

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Educational Use

MedicoHub Connect supports education, structured questions, article reading, and communication follow-up. It does not replace direct medical consultation, examination, diagnosis, prescription, or emergency care.

Emergency Warning

Do not use the app for emergencies. For new weakness, slurred speech, double vision, severe headache, inability to walk, fainting, chest pain, sudden hearing loss, or any urgent symptom, seek local emergency medical help.

User Responsibilities

  • Use respectful, lawful, and truthful communication.
  • Do not upload content you do not have permission to share.
  • Do not post spam, harassment, misleading medical claims, or dangerous advice.
  • Keep your login credentials private.

Moderation

Administrators and doctors may moderate content to protect safety, privacy, and service integrity. Public educational threads may be edited, hidden, or removed if they are unsafe, misleading, abusive, or inappropriate.
