# Community and Referrals

## Implemented Foundation

Growth storage now includes referral buckets and dashboard metrics for referral registrations and activations once events are ingested.

## Ethical Referral Rules

- Reward activation, not raw clicks.
- Do not expose referred-user identity or activity without consent.
- Do not offer clinical priority, prescriptions, diagnosis, or care access as referral rewards.
- Add fraud controls before incentives: rate limits, duplicate-account checks, self-referral prevention.
