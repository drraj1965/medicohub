Community safety

Content Policy

Allowed content

Educational questions, doctor-authored articles, rehabilitation guidance, and respectful health discussion are allowed.

Not allowed

  • Emergency requests that require immediate local care.
  • Abusive, discriminatory, sexual, illegal, or harassing content.
  • Miracle-cure claims, unsafe medical advice, spam, scams, or misleading advertising.
  • Sharing another person’s private medical information without permission.

Moderation

Doctors and administrators may remove or hide unsafe or inappropriate content.
