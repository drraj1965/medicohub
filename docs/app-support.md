## Account Deletion

Users can delete their account inside the app:

1. Sign in to MedicoHub Connect.
2. Open Settings.
3. Select Delete Account.
4. Enter the current password and type DELETE to confirm.

This removes the Firebase sign-in account and MedicoHub profile. Question threads authored by the deleted account are removed from public lists.

## Safety Notice

MedicoHub Connect is for education and communication support only. It does not replace emergency care, in-person medical assessment, or professional medical advice from a licensed clinician.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
